Our Story

A Family Still Growing

FOUNTAIN OF CHRIST RESTORATION MINISTRIES started with a handful of families praying together in a living room, asking God for their block. Years on, we're still that same family — we've just grown a lot bigger.

We exist to help people find their way back to God, to each other, and to the purpose they were created for. However you found us, there's a seat saved for you this Sunday.

Worship Freely

Every service makes room for honest, unhurried worship — no performance, just presence.

Grow Deeply

Through teaching and discipleship, we help each other build a faith that holds under pressure.

Serve Boldly

We believe restored people restore places, so we give our time to our neighbours, not just their problems.
